About the Academy

New batches starting now at Harikrishna Complex

ALEX English Speaking Courses runs from Harikrishna Complex, above Sankalp Computers, Bhaji Market, Chandannagar, Pune – 411014. The promise is simple: quitters never win, and winners never quit — join, stay with it, and speak. Eligibility is open to anyone who has passed at least 7th standard.

Who joins? All students, housewives, officers, doctors, engineers, working professionals and anyone else who wants to speak English with confidence.

  • Eligibility: minimum 7th standard pass — no other qualification needed
  • Switch between any of the 4 daily batch timings whenever it suits you
  • Enquiry & admission open Morning 7–12 and Evening 4–9, every day
  • If you complete the course and still can't speak English, your fee is refunded — but latecomers are not given admission
